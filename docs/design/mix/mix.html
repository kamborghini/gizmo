Reactor, a direction

The mix

Calm's quiet sheet, Data-forward's figures and Brand-bold's focal points. Explanations wait behind small info buttons.

From Calm workspace

  • One white sheet; only the band and the counters are boxed
  • Sections are a title and space; lists are rows split by hairlines
  • Teal marks what is chosen, links and the one primary action
  • A line between orders instead of a box round each one

From Data-forward

  • Every change carries an arrow, not just a colour
  • Taken, Still expected and Plan keep one colour on every screen
  • Chart details on hover or tap, and a Compare methods switch
  • One How it works button holds the method pages

From Brand-bold

  • An ink frame around the sheet
  • Forecast's month figure leads, its range drawn as one bar
  • Queue counters you can read across the bench
  • Bricolage for page titles; an icon tile on each band label

The Forecast in the Guide

Why £53,691

Big orders counted apart

Each month is split into orders under £2,000 and big orders. The smaller ones follow last year's same month, scaled by how this year is trading. The big ones are taken as their average month over the last year.

It misleads when big orders start arriving much more or less often than last year.
