# Rows that open, tick rows, two small fixes Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** No row is a pretend button any more (the Inbox, the queue, CRM Contacts and deals), every tick row is built by `tickRow()` with the body text, Memory's read pages get a finger-sized line and Customise marks its blocks as list items; the record's nested, target-size and list findings fall.

**Architecture:** A new section `static/src/js/part-row.js` (after `js/part-field.js`) holds `rowOpener`, `opensOnClick` and `tickRow`. The rows keep their look: the hidden opener's focus draws the row's existing ring through `:has()`.

**Tech Stack:** as the last three pieces.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-rows-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- The queue (`.lbl-qrow`): only its role, tab stop and key handler go; its content, columns, statuses, actions and look are unchanged, and its screenshots must be identical before and after.
- Visible change only in dispatch's notify and insurance ticks' words (body text) and Memory's read-page links' line height.
- The record may not rise; it is lowered where findings fall.
- A release note with the first app change; no agents (self-review); branch `refactor/tables-rows` stacked on `refactor/switches-fields`.

## Review Focus

1. **Enter on a row's opener in the Inbox** must open that email once, not also the j/k cursor's row through the page-wide shortcut. Pinned by Task 1's harness test (`rowOpener` stops Enter and Space at the button).
2. **A click on a row's own tick, Claim, link or action** must do only that, never also open the row. Pinned by Task 1's harness test for `opensOnClick`.
3. **The keyboard path to the queue's preview** after its rows stop being tab stops: the Preview button. Pinned by a keyboard journey (Task 2): Tab reaches a row's Preview, Enter opens the preview.
4. **The focus ring when the opener has focus**: a keyboard user must see which row they are on. Pinned by Task 2's page-suite check that each converted row family's focus rule includes `:has(.row-open:focus-visible)`.
5. **A tick row's words changing size elsewhere**: only dispatch's two families were 12 grey. Pinned by the screenshot comparison (Task 4).

### Task 1: The row parts

**Files:** `static/src/js/part-row.js` (create; manifest after `js/part-field.js`), `static/src/css/` (the `.tick-row` rule and the `.row-open` rule), `data/changelog.json`, `tests/test_frontend.py`

**Interfaces:**
- `rowOpener(label: string, onOpen: () => void) -> HTMLButtonElement`: class `row-open sr-only`, `aria-label` = label, its click calls `onOpen` and stops there, its keydown stops Enter and Space from leaving it.
- `opensOnClick(row: HTMLElement, onOpen: () => void) -> void`: a click on `row` calls `onOpen` unless the click began inside a `button, a, input, select, textarea, label` inside the row.
- `tickRow(cls?: string) -> HTMLLabelElement`: class `tick-row` plus `cls`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests.** `t_a_row_opens_from_its_own_button_and_its_controls_keep_theirs` (node, `MINIDOM` + `row_part()`): `rowOpener('Open: X', fn)` has class `row-open sr-only` and `aria-label` `Open: X`; its click calls `fn` once and the click does not reach the row; a keydown Enter dispatched on it does not reach a `document` listener (stopped); `opensOnClick(row, fn)`: a click on the row calls `fn`, a click on a tick inside it does not. `t_a_tick_row_is_one_recipe`: `tickRow('tm-tabpick').className == 'tick-row tm-tabpick'`; the CSS has `.tick-row {` with `font-size: var(--text-body)` and `color: var(--text-primary)`; neither `.disp-notify {` nor `.disp-insure {` sets `font-size` or `color`.
- [ ] **Step 2: Build.** The part (markers `/* ---------- the row parts ----------` / `… end ---------- */`), `row_part()` helper in the page suite, the `.tick-row` rule (flex, centred, gap `--sp-2`, body text, primary ink, pointer), the family rules lose their own font-size and colour, the release note ("Rows and ticks": "Rows that open something are proper controls for a screen reader, the dispatch ticks use the same words as every other, and Memory's links are easier to tap.").
- [ ] **Step 3: Run** the two tests and the page suite. **Commit.**
